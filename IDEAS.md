# 🐾 Waystones Pets Addon – Feature Ideas

A collection of enhancements and creative ideas to expand your addon for teleporting tamed pets with Waystones.

---

## ✅ Existing Core Concept
- Teleports tamed pets when using a Waystone, removing the need for leads.

---

## 🐾 Pet Selection & Management

### 🔹 Shift + Use Selection
- Open a GUI when shift+using a Waystone to select pets.
- List nearby tamed pets with checkboxes.
- Support persistent selection storage.

### 🔹 Pet Groups
- Allow players to create custom-named groups (e.g., "Exploration Team").
- Select a group to teleport instead of picking each pet manually.

### 🔹 Persistent Selection
- Save selected pets between teleports and game sessions.

---

## 🧠 Smart Features

### 🔹 Auto-Follow Toggle
- Enable a setting per pet to always teleport with the player.

### 🔹 Distance Filtering
- Only include pets within a configurable radius.
- Warn players about pets left behind.

### 🔹 Cooldowns & Limits
- Prevent pets from being teleported too frequently.
- Configurable cooldowns per pet or Waystone.

### 🔹 XP or Item Costs
- Require XP levels or custom items (e.g. "Pet Warp Token") to teleport pets.

---

## 🎨 GUI & Visual Enhancements

### 🔹 Pet Portraits & Info
- Display pet icons and names in the GUI.
- Show hover info: health, sit/stand status, dimension, etc.

### 🔹 Teleport Effects
- Particles and sound effects when pets teleport in/out.

---

## 🐕 Pet Behavior Expansion

### 🔹 Command Items
- Items like a “Pet Whistle” to send nearby pets commands: follow, stay, sit.

### 🔹 Pet Recall Stone
- An item that recalls your pets from a distance or to a saved location.

### 🔹 Safe Arrival Checks
- Avoid teleporting pets into lava or blocks; find safe landing zones near destination.

---

## 🌐 Multiplayer & Server Support

### 🔹 Player-Specific Data
- Keep pet teleport preferences per-player on multiplayer servers.

### 🔹 Permissions Integration
- Optional permission checks for advanced features.

### 🔹 Cross-Dimensional Handling
- Graceful fallback if cross-dimension teleport fails.

---

## ⚙️ Config & Mod Integration

### 🔹 Config Options
- Enable/disable pet teleport
- Adjust max range, costs, cooldowns
- GUI toggle

### 🔹 Waystone Upgrade System
- Add a special “pet module” to Waystones that enables pet teleportation.

### 🔹 Curios Support
- Add a “Pet Charm” item that acts as a portable pet-link for teleporting.

---

## 🧩 Mod Compatibility

- **Alex’s Mobs**
- **Doggy Talents**
- **Sophisticated Wolves**
- Add detection and compatibility patches for pets from other mods.

---

## ✨ Future Ideas
- Pet path visualization before teleport
- Pet memories or bonding effects
- Tamable mounts teleport logic
